Heartbeat of Nigeria
Living History

Where Antiquity Meets Modernity

Edo State is internationally recognized as the birthplace of the ancient Benin Empire, famous for its magnificent bronze castings, intricate ivory carvings, and royal traditions that remain active to this day.

From the bustling bronze smith quarters of Igun Street to the lush landscapes of Okomu National Park and the scenic hills of Somorika, Edo offers an unforgettable blend of historical exploration and eco-adventure.

Top Destinations & Landmarks

Discover the premier historic, natural, and cultural attractions across Edo State.

Monuments Oredo, Benin City

Palace of the Oba of Benin

Located in the heart of Benin City, the Royal Palace is a sacred site housing architectural relics and royal artifacts dating back to the 13th century.

Guided tours with permission
Craftsmanship Igun Street, Benin

Igun Street Bronze Casting

A UNESCO-listed cultural heritage site home to traditional bronze casters practicing lost-wax casting methods passed down for generations.

Authentic souvenirs available
Eco-Tourism Ovia South-West

Okomu National Park

A tropical rainforest sanctuary protecting endangered white-throated monkeys, forest elephants, canopy walkways, and rich bird life.

Canopy walk & lodge available

Somorika Hills & Boulders

Famous for its massive granite rock formations, ancient healing traditions, panoramic views, and scenic hiking terrain in northern Edo.

Ideal for hiking & photography

Plan Your Trip to Edo State

By Air

Benin Airport (BNI) connects directly to major national hubs including Lagos and Abuja.

By Road

Connected via major interstate expressways linking South-South, South-West, and Northern states.

Hospitality

Dedicated tourist assistance, police security, and municipal guide services available upon request.
