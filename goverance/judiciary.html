Judicial Structure

Courts & Judicial Divisions

The court system is structured across multiple tiers to serve civil, criminal, and customary matters throughout Edo State.

High Court of Justice

Holds unlimited jurisdiction over civil and criminal proceedings. Headquartered in Benin City with judicial divisions across key local councils.

Benin City Auchi & Ekpoma

Customary Court of Appeal

Exercises appellate and supervisory jurisdiction in civil proceedings involving questions of customary law across the state.

Customary Jurisprudence

Magistrate Courts

Handles summary criminal trials, summary civil claims, and preliminary inquiries situated across all 18 Local Government Areas.

18 LGAs Coverage

Area & Customary Courts

Adjudicates local disputes, family matters, land tenure disputes, and traditional law issues at the community level.

Grassroots Access

Multi-Door Court House

Provides court-connected Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) including arbitration, conciliation, and mediation.

Fast-Track Settlement

Judicial Service Commission

Oversees appointments, promotions, training, and disciplinary control of judicial officers and support personnel.

Judicial Oversight
